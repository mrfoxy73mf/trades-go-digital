# TGD Trades competitor pricing research

Research date: 29 October 2026

This is an internal research note. It is not published on the website.

## Competitor pricing snapshot

| App | Starting price | Higher plans | Pricing model and notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| ServiceM8 | Free, then £25/month | £59–£269/month | Paid plans include unlimited users but apply monthly job limits. The £25 Starter plan includes 50 new jobs per month. |
| Powered Now | £28/month | £32–£40 per user/month | Business is for one user. Higher plans add certificates, forms, a customer portal, AI features and team tools. |
| Workever | £29 per user/month | £39 per user/month | The higher plan adds forms, stock, assets, timesheets and a customer portal. |
| Tradify | £34 per user/month | £37–£44 per user/month | Features increase across Lite, Pro and Plus plans. |
| Fergus | From approximately £35/month | Higher plan available | Month-to-month, per-user pricing. |
| Re-flow | £39 per user/month | — | Minimum of ten users and aimed at larger field teams. |

## Provisional TGD Trades pricing idea

- Solo: £19.99/month for one user.
- Business: £29.99/month including three users.
- Additional users: approximately £6–£8 each per month.
- Annual payment: two months free.
- Trial: 30 days with no payment card required.
- Early adopters: £14.99/month for the first year.

## Reasoning

This would position TGD Trades below Powered Now, Tradify and Workever while recognising the value of its customer portal, certificates, supplier paperwork, van stock, wages, CIS, VAT records, accountant reports and AI Mailroom.

ServiceM8 is the strongest price competitor because its £25 paid plan permits unlimited users, although it limits the number of new jobs. TGD Trades should avoid £30–£40 per-user pricing at launch while the product is new.

Before fixing the final price, calculate the expected monthly cost of storage, AI document processing, email, support, payment processing and any included messages for each active customer.

## Next research step

Create a complete TGD Trades feature inventory and compare every feature with the competitors above. Use that comparison to decide which features belong in Solo, Business and any future higher plan.
